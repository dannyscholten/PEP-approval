Prepare your PEP for approval

Build your proposed 120 EC Personal Education Plan and generate a PDF for preapproval. The tool checks your entries; academic approval is given by the relevant reviewers.

  1. Start or import a plan

    Complete your personal details, specialisation, path (if applicable) and previous education. Already used the Personal Education Plan Builder? Choose Export for approval there, then Open draft here.

  2. Complete your programme

    The light-blue tables contain the courses included in your PEP. Use + Add to open the available choices and add electives or optional projects. Use Edit details to enter a project’s registration route, proposed or confirmed host and topic status in a popup. Complete all fields marked with a red *.

  3. Review, save and generate the PDF

    Use Export for approval to check the EC total and outstanding items. Download PDF for approval becomes available when required details and blocking checks are resolved, including justified exception requests where offered. Decision and verification items remain for the reviewers. Use Save draft to save your work; changes are not saved automatically.

  4. Send the PDF by e-mail to your specialisation coordinator

    E-mail the completed PDF to your coordinator for review and signing for preapproval. If an Admissions Board decision on Principles courses is required, the coordinator arranges that review and signature first.

  5. Submit for final approval

    After coordinator preapproval, submit the signed PEP to the DDS examination board via VU keuzeruimte — submit for examination-board approval. Coordinator preapproval is not final examination-board approval.
